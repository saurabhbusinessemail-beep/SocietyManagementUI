## 2. Executive Summary
- A comprehensive platform for managing residential societies.
- Integrated modules for Admins, Managers, Residents, and Security.
- Real-time communication and automated financial tracking.
- Secure document management and approval workflows.

## 3. Dashboard & Quick Overview
- **Key Features**:
  - Global Search (Society/Flat/Member).
  - Quick access to active modules.
  - Subscription status and plan duration at a glance.
  - Real-time notifications for critical updates.

## 4. Society Management
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Society Profile: Name, GPS location, and contact details.
  - Role-based Management: Admins and Managers assignment.
  - Plan Monitoring: View active subscription and features.
  - Multi-Society Support: Manage multiple societies from a single account.

---

## 5. Building & Flat Hierarchy
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Building Management: Grouping flats by blocks/wings.
  - Flat Inventory: Track total vs. remaining flat capacity.
  - Bulk Creation: Automated generation of flats by floor.
  - Visual Sorting: Easy navigation through building blocks.

---

## 6. Flat Details & Resident Management
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Resident Profiles: Detailed view of Owners and Tenants.
  - Residency Type: Self-occupied, Rented, or Vacant status.
  - Family/Member Management: Add/Remove additional family members.
  - Real-time Status: Track if a flat is currently active or terminated.

---

## 7. Tenant Verification & Documents
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Document Upload: Securely store ID proofs and lease agreements.
  - Approval Workflow: Admin review and approval/rejection of documents.
  - Status Tracking: Pending, Approved, or Rejected status with reason.
  - Audit Trail: Track who approved/uploaded documents and when.

---

## 8. Maintenance & Rent Management
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Payment Recording: Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, or Cheque.
  - Approval Workflow: Owners/Admins record, and counterparties approve.
  - Payment History: Month-by-month tracking for each flat.
  - Automatic Reminders: Ensure timely payments with status indicators.

---

## 9. Complaint Resolution Module
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Categorization: Public vs. Private complaints.
  - Priority Levels: Low, Medium, High, and Urgent.
  - Status Workflow: Submitted -> In Progress -> Resolved -> Closed.
  - Interactive Comments: Real-time discussion between resident and admin.

## 10. Integrated Chat System
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Contextual Rooms: General society chat, Building-level, or Flat-specific.
  - Private Messaging: One-on-one communication.
  - Real-time Synchronization: Delta-based caching for performance.
  - Search & Filters: Quickly find messages or chat rooms.

---

## 11. Security & Gate Management
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Gate Entry Logs: Record visitors, deliveries, and service providers.
  - Gate Pass (OTP): Secure verification for expected visitors.
  - History Tracking: Full audit trail of every entry and exit.
  - Real-time Alerts: Residents get notified of visitors at the gate.

---

## 12. Parking Management
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Slot Allocation: Mapping parking spots to specific flats.
  - Type Categorization: 2W, 4W, etc.
  - Availability Tracking: Monitor occupied vs. vacant parking spaces.
  - Building Context: Filter parking by building or society.

---

## 13. Subscription & Pricing
- **Functional Overview**:
  - Tiered Plans: Different features based on society size and needs.
  - Duration-based Badges: Standardized visual indicators for plan status.
  - Automated Billing: Transparent pricing and plan upgrades.
  - Feature Locking: Dynamic UI based on active subscription.

---

## 14. Technical Stack
- **Frontend**: Angular, Ionic (Capacitor for Mobile).
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, MongoDB (Mongoose).
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS with standardized Design Tokens.
- **Real-time**: Socket.io for Chat and Notifications.
